Frequently asked questions

The short answers to the questions we hear most. Can't find yours? Contact us.

Orders & shipping

How long does delivery take?
We dispatch within 24–48 hours. Metros usually receive orders in 3–4 days; the rest of India in 5–7 days. You'll get a tracking link by SMS, email and WhatsApp the moment your order ships.
Is shipping free?
Yes, on all orders above ₹999. Below that, a flat ₹79 shipping fee applies.
Do you offer Cash on Delivery?
Yes — COD is available across most of India. You can pay by cash or UPI at your doorstep.
Can I change my address after ordering?
If the order hasn't shipped yet, yes — message us on WhatsApp with your order number and the new address.

Returns & exchanges

What is your return policy?
7 days from delivery for a return or size exchange. Items must be unworn, unwashed, with tags on. We arrange free doorstep pickup.
The size doesn't fit — what now?
Request a size exchange from your order page (or WhatsApp us). We pick up the original and ship the new size as soon as the pickup is scanned — you don't wait for the return to reach us.
When do I get my refund?
Refunds go back to your original payment method within 5–7 working days of the quality check. COD orders are refunded to your UPI or bank account.

Products & sizing

How do I pick my size?
Every product page has a size chart with garment measurements in inches. Measure a similar garment you own and compare — if you're between sizes, take the larger one.
Will the colours look exactly like the photos?
We photograph in natural light and don't over-edit, but screens vary — expect minor differences, especially on hand-dyed and block-printed pieces, where small variations are the nature of the craft.
How should I wash my Vawehall pieces?
Gentle machine wash cold with like colours, dry in shade, warm iron. Embroidered and printed pieces prefer being ironed inside-out. Full care details are on every product page.
