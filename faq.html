Frequently asked questions

The short answers to the questions we hear most. Can't find yours? Contact us.

Orders & shipping

How long does delivery take?
We ship within 24–48 hours from our workshop. Delivery across the United States typically takes 5–10 business days. You'll get a tracking link by email and text the moment your order ships.
Is shipping free?
Yes, on all US orders above $50. Below that, a flat $4.99 shipping fee applies.
What payment methods do you accept?
All major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover), PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and buy-now-pay-later with Klarna or Afterpay. Payments are processed securely by our payment gateway — we never see or store your card details.
Can I change my address after ordering?
If the order hasn't shipped yet, yes — email us with your order number and the new address.
Will I have to pay customs or import fees?
No — the price you see at checkout is the price you pay. We handle any customs paperwork and fees for US orders.

Returns & exchanges

What is your return policy?
7 days from delivery for a return or size exchange. Items must be unworn, unwashed, with tags on. We email you a prepaid return label — drop the package at any carrier location.
The size doesn't fit — what now?
Request a size exchange from your order confirmation email (or just email us). We ship the new size as soon as your return is scanned by the carrier — you don't wait for it to reach us.
When do I get my refund?
Refunds go back to your original payment method within 5–7 business days of the quality check.

Products & sizing

How do I pick my size?
Every product page has a size chart with garment measurements in inches. Measure a similar garment you own and compare — if you're between sizes, take the larger one.
Will the colours look exactly like the photos?
We photograph in natural light and don't over-edit, but screens vary — expect minor differences, especially on hand-dyed and block-printed pieces, where small variations are the nature of the craft.
How should I wash my Vawehall pieces?
Gentle machine wash cold with like colours, dry in shade, warm iron. Embroidered and printed pieces prefer being ironed inside-out. Full care details are on every product page.
