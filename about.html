Our story

Clothes made the long, honest way.

Vawehall started with a simple irritation: good fabric shouldn't be this hard to find at a fair price.

We began as a small workshop sourcing fabric directly from mills and block-printers, cutting out the layers of middlemen that quietly double the price of a kurta before it reaches you. Every piece we make is designed in-house, made in India, and priced from the cost sheet up — not from an inflated MRP down.

What we stand for

  • Honest fabric. Real GSM numbers on every tee, real fibre content on every label. No mystery blends.
  • Fair pricing. Our discount is not theatre — the MRP is what the market charges; our price is what the garment honestly costs plus a fair margin.
  • Made responsibly. Small batches, audited workshops, azo-free dyes, and packaging without single-use plastic wherever possible.
  • Fit for India. Cuts graded on Indian body measurements, fabrics chosen for Indian weather.

The road ahead

We're a young brand with a short catalog and long ambitions. New drops land every few weeks — kurtas for the festive calendar, heavyweight tees year-round, and a denim program that's just getting started. If you're reading this, you're early. We like that.
