Store policies

Shipping, returns, privacy and terms — everything in one place.

Important: these are starter drafts to show structure. Shipping policy

  • Orders are dispatched within 24–48 hours of confirmation (excluding Sundays and public holidays).
  • Delivery typically takes 3–4 days in metro cities and 5–7 days elsewhere in India.
  • Shipping is free on orders above ₹999; a flat fee of ₹79 applies below that.
  • Cash on Delivery is available on serviceable pincodes.
  • Tracking details are shared by email, SMS and WhatsApp as soon as the order ships.

Return & exchange policy

  • Returns and size exchanges are accepted within 7 days of delivery.
  • Items must be unworn, unwashed and undamaged, with all original tags and packaging.
  • We arrange free reverse pickup from your doorstep; where pickup isn't serviceable, we'll guide you to self-ship and reimburse courier charges up to ₹100.
  • Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5–7 working days after quality check; COD refunds go to your UPI or bank account.
  • For hygiene reasons, certain categories (e.g. innerwear, if added later) may be non-returnable — such items will be marked on their product page.

Privacy policy

  • We collect only what's needed to fulfil your order: name, delivery address, phone number, and email.
  • Payment details are processed entirely by our payment gateway partner — we never see or store your card number or UPI PIN.
  • We do not sell your personal data. Order data is shared only with delivery partners to complete your shipment.
  • You may request a copy or deletion of your data at any time by writing to care@vawehall.com, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
  • This site uses basic analytics cookies to understand what shoppers browse; you can block cookies in your browser without affecting checkout.

Terms of service

  • All prices are in Indian Rupees and inclusive of all applicable taxes (GST).
  • Product colours may vary slightly due to screen differences and the handmade nature of some fabrics.
  • Placing an order constitutes an offer to purchase; we reserve the right to cancel orders affected by pricing errors or stock issues, with a full refund.
  • All content on this site — designs, photographs, text and the Vawehall name — is our intellectual property and may not be reproduced without permission.
  • Disputes, if any, are subject to the jurisdiction of the courts of [your registered city], India.
