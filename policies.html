Store policies

Shipping, returns, privacy and terms — everything in one place.

Important: these are starter drafts to show structure. Before going live, have them reviewed and completed with your real business details — US consumer-protection and privacy rules require clear return terms and an honest privacy policy. See the docs/ folder in this project (especially the USA-market addendum) for the full legal checklist.

Shipping policy

  • Orders ship within 24–48 hours of confirmation (excluding Sundays and public holidays).
  • Delivery across the United States typically takes 5–10 business days, fully tracked.
  • Shipping is free on orders above $50; a flat fee of $4.99 applies below that.
  • The price at checkout is final — we handle all customs paperwork and import fees on your behalf (delivered duty paid).
  • Tracking details are shared by email and text as soon as the order ships.

Return & exchange policy

  • Returns and size exchanges are accepted within 7 days of delivery.
  • Items must be unworn, unwashed and undamaged, with all original tags and packaging.
  • We email you a prepaid return label — drop the package at any carrier location; exchanges ship as soon as the return is scanned.
  • Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5–7 business days after quality check.
  • For hygiene reasons, certain categories (e.g. innerwear, if added later) may be non-returnable — such items will be marked on their product page.

Privacy policy

  • We collect only what's needed to fulfil your order: name, delivery address, phone number, and email.
  • Payment details are processed entirely by our payment gateway partner — we never see or store your card number.
  • We do not sell your personal data. Order data is shared only with delivery partners to complete your shipment.
  • You may request a copy or deletion of your data at any time by writing to care@vawehall.com. California residents have additional rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right to know, delete, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information.
  • Marketing emails are sent only if you opt in, and every one includes a working unsubscribe link (as required by the CAN-SPAM Act).
  • This site uses basic analytics cookies to understand what shoppers browse; you can block cookies in your browser without affecting checkout.

Terms of service

  • All prices are in US Dollars. Applicable sales tax, where required, is shown at checkout.
  • Product colours may vary slightly due to screen differences and the handmade nature of some fabrics.
  • Placing an order constitutes an offer to purchase; we reserve the right to cancel orders affected by pricing errors or stock issues, with a full refund.
  • Our garments are designed and made in India — fiber content, country of origin and care instructions are stated on every product label as required by US textile labeling rules.
  • All content on this site — designs, photographs, text and the Vawehall name — is our intellectual property and may not be reproduced without permission.
  • These terms are governed by the laws of [your registered jurisdiction — confirm with your legal advisor].

Questions & complaints

Something not right? We want to know:

  • Email: care@vawehall.com — we acknowledge within 48 hours and aim to resolve within 7 days.
  • Order issues: include your order number (starts with VW-) so we can help faster.
